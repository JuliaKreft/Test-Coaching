Short bio

Julia Kreft is a coach & speaker for inner transformation and a senior leader in finance (Director Finance Excellence & Transformation at Syntegon). As a certified business & life coach, she combines leadership experience from global companies (P&G, Horváth) with the depth of real personal development. She speaks about leadership, psychological safety, self-worth and the human side of transformation — including at the Finance Summit “The Future of Finance”.
